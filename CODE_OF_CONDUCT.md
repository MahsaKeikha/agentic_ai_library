# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to making participation in the Agentic AI Library respectful, constructive, inclusive, and professionally useful for researchers, engineers, students, educators, practitioners, and contributors from different backgrounds and disciplines.

## Expected behavior

Participants are expected to:

- communicate respectfully and professionally
- critique ideas, code, evidence, and architecture rather than people
- welcome good-faith questions and differing technical perspectives
- acknowledge uncertainty and correct mistakes openly
- respect privacy, confidentiality, attribution, and intellectual property
- avoid harassment, intimidation, discrimination, personal attacks, or demeaning language
- avoid spam, deceptive promotion, manipulation, or coordinated abuse
- keep safety-sensitive discussions focused on responsible engineering and legitimate defensive or research purposes

## Unacceptable behavior

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, threats, discriminatory conduct, sexualized harassment, doxxing, deliberate disclosure of private information, repeated bad-faith disruption, impersonation, plagiarism, malicious code contributions, secret harvesting, or attempts to weaken safety and authorization controls for abusive purposes.

## Technical disagreement

Strong disagreement is welcome when it improves the work. Contributors should explain assumptions, evidence, tradeoffs, and failure modes. Maintainers may ask that claims of performance, safety, compliance, or production readiness be supported by reproducible evidence.

## Scope

This code applies to project repositories, issues, pull requests, reviews, discussions, and other spaces where someone is representing participation in the project.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions, comments, issues, or other participation that violates this code. Repeated or severe violations may result in temporary or permanent restrictions from project spaces.

## Reporting

If you experience or observe conduct that violates this policy, report it privately to the repository owner through an appropriate verified channel. Security vulnerabilities should instead follow `SECURITY.md`.

## Attribution

This policy is inspired by widely used open-source community standards while being tailored to the engineering, research, and safety goals of the Agentic AI Library.
