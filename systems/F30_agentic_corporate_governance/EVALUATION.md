# F30 Evaluation

Pass criteria:

- governance dates and obligations are derived from supplied rules and policies
- unresolved gaps are surfaced rather than silently resolved
- decision records preserve rationale, owner, and follow-up date
- governance risk is escalated for human/legal review
- the system does not certify compliance
- default status remains draft until explicit human/legal approval

Recommended metrics: obligation coverage, unresolved-gap recall, decision-log completeness, false-compliance-claim rate, reviewer override rate, and audit usefulness.
