## Public-sector boundary

These reference workflows do not exercise government authority, make eligibility or enforcement decisions, target individuals, conduct operational surveillance, generate tactical military plans, manipulate voters, or autonomously publish official statements. Systems involving defense, intelligence, elections, emergency management, and public health are restricted to high-level analysis, open/public information, governance, planning, and human-reviewed decision support.

## Shared requirements

Each system must preserve source provenance where available, explicit uncertainty, alternative hypotheses where relevant, public-interest and equity review, privacy protections, auditability, and an authorized-human gate before official publication or consequential action.
