# F28 Evaluation

Pass criteria:

- customer and problem statements remain hypotheses until evidence supports them
- MVP scope includes explicit non-goals
- experiments are falsifiable and have success/stop criteria
- fundraising readiness identifies evidence gaps rather than manufacturing traction
