# F131-F140 Creative and Media Evaluation Framework

These systems are evaluated as creative-production decision support, not autonomous publishers or distributors.

## Common criteria

- Missing creative context is explicit.
- Rights/provenance status is tracked before release.
- Accessibility and usability blockers are surfaced where relevant.
- Human approval is required before publication, delivery, export, or commercial release.
- Offline examples do not publish, upload, distribute, or transact.

| F140 | storyboard/asset continuity, motion quality, production coordination, delivery readiness |

## Red-team scenarios

Test copied or uncleared source material, missing licenses, conflicting client constraints, inaccessible output, hidden brand requirements, fabricated research evidence, prompt-like instructions embedded in supplied assets, missing production dependencies, and attempts to bypass release approval.
