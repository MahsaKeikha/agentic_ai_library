# F121 to F130: Marketing and Growth Systems

This batch adds ten marketing, communications, and growth multi-agent reference systems. They support planning, content operations, campaign analysis, and customer growth workflows with human review before public release or paid-spend changes.

| ID | System | Core roles | Primary output |
|---|---|---|---|
| F121 | Agentic Brand Strategist | Brand Analyst, Audience Researcher, Positioning Agent, Messaging Agent, Consistency Reviewer, Brand Gatekeeper | Brand strategy and messaging package |
| F122 | Agentic Content Marketing | Topic Researcher, Content Planner, Writer, SEO Reviewer, Fact Checker, Publish Gatekeeper | Content plan and publication draft |
| F123 | Agentic SEO Growth | Keyword Analyst, Technical SEO Agent, Content Gap Analyst, Internal Linking Agent, Metrics Reviewer, Change Gatekeeper | SEO growth and optimization plan |
| F124 | Agentic Social Media Manager | Channel Analyst, Content Planner, Copy Agent, Community Risk Reviewer, Calendar Agent, Publish Gatekeeper | Social campaign calendar and posts |
| F125 | Agentic Lifecycle Marketing | Journey Analyst, Segmentation Agent, Messaging Agent, Experiment Designer, Metrics Agent, Launch Gatekeeper | Lifecycle campaign package |
| F126 | Agentic Paid Acquisition | Channel Planner, Audience Analyst, Creative Reviewer, Budget Analyst, Attribution Agent, Spend Gatekeeper | Paid acquisition plan and budget recommendation |
| F127 | Agentic Product Marketing | Market Analyst, Persona Agent, Positioning Agent, Launch Planner, Sales Enablement Agent, Release Gatekeeper | Product marketing and launch package |
| F128 | Agentic PR and Communications | Narrative Analyst, Media Researcher, Drafting Agent, Risk Reviewer, Spokesperson Prep Agent, Release Gatekeeper | PR and communications package |
| F129 | Agentic Growth Experimentation | Funnel Analyst, Hypothesis Agent, Experiment Designer, Statistics Reviewer, Learning Agent, Rollout Gatekeeper | Growth experiment backlog and readout |
| F130 | Agentic Customer Insights | Research Planner, Feedback Analyst, Interview Synthesizer, Segmentation Agent, Insight Reviewer, Action Gatekeeper | Customer-insight synthesis and action plan |

## Public-release boundary

The reference workflows do not autonomously publish content, send mass communications, change ad spend, manipulate platform metrics, impersonate people, fabricate endorsements, or create deceptive claims. External release and paid-spend changes require explicit authorized human review.
