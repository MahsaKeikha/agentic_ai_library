## Responsible-use boundary

These systems support educators and institutions; they do not replace instructors, academic committees, institutional review, disability/accessibility offices, or due-process procedures. Academic-integrity outputs must never be treated as automatic findings of misconduct.

## Shared requirements

Every system must preserve explicit evidence status, traceable workflow state, human review before publication/submission or disciplinary action, deterministic offline examples, tests for missing evidence and gate behavior, and system-specific evaluation criteria.
