# F151 to F160: Finance and Risk Systems

This batch adds ten finance, treasury, banking, insurance, and risk-management multi-agent reference systems. These workflows support analysis and governance. They do not autonomously execute trades, move money, approve credit, bind insurance coverage, or file taxes.

| ID | System | Core roles | Primary output |
|---|---|---|---|
| F151 | Agentic Investment Research | Thesis Analyst, Financial Analyst, Industry Analyst, Valuation Reviewer, Risk Agent, Evidence Auditor | Investment research memo |
| F152 | Agentic Portfolio Manager | Allocation Analyst, Exposure Agent, Scenario Analyst, Risk Reviewer, Constraint Agent, Rebalance Gatekeeper | Portfolio review and rebalance proposal |
| F153 | Agentic Quantitative Trading Research | Data Analyst, Signal Researcher, Backtest Reviewer, Bias/Leakage Agent, Risk Analyst, Research Gatekeeper | Research-only strategy evaluation |
| F154 | Agentic Insurance Operations | Intake Analyst, Coverage Workflow Agent, Claims Triage Agent, Fraud/Risk Reviewer, Compliance Agent, Human Decision Gate | Insurance operations package |
| F155 | Agentic Banking Assistant | Service Analyst, Product Information Agent, Transaction Reviewer, Fraud Escalation Agent, Compliance Reviewer, Human Gate | Banking service and review package |
| F156 | Agentic Tax Planning | Fact Organizer, Jurisdiction Agent, Scenario Analyst, Documentation Agent, Risk Reviewer, Professional Review Gate | Tax-planning worksheet and questions |
| F157 | Agentic Enterprise Risk Manager | Risk Identifier, Quantification Agent, Control Reviewer, Scenario Planner, Risk Aggregator, Executive Gatekeeper | Enterprise risk register and treatment plan |
| F158 | Agentic Treasury Operations | Cash Analyst, Liquidity Planner, Forecast Agent, Counterparty Risk Reviewer, Controls Agent, Payment Gatekeeper | Treasury and liquidity review package |
| F159 | Agentic Credit Analyst | Borrower Analyst, Financial Reviewer, Cash-Flow Agent, Collateral/Structure Reviewer, Risk Agent, Credit Committee Gate | Credit analysis package |
| F160 | Agentic ESG Reporting | Boundary Analyst, Data Quality Agent, Metrics Agent, Disclosure Reviewer, Assurance Readiness Agent, Publication Gatekeeper | ESG reporting and evidence package |

## Financial boundary

Outputs are research, planning, documentation, and decision-support artifacts. The offline workflows do not execute securities trades, transfer funds, extend or deny credit, issue insurance coverage decisions, submit tax returns, or provide personalized financial, investment, legal, or tax advice.
