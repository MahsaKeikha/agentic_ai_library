# F50 IoT Engineering Architecture

F50 separates device onboarding, telemetry, edge processing, connectivity, and fleet operations into specialist agents. Tools provide deterministic operations, while skills hold reusable IoT engineering procedures. The orchestrator coordinates work through explicit state, memory, safety controls, tracing, and evaluation.

The system is designed for engineering planning and review. Human approval remains required for fleet rollout, rollback, credential, and physical deployment decisions.
