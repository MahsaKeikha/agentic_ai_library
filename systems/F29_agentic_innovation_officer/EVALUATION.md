# F29 Evaluation

Pass criteria:

- opportunities are tied to supplied signals and strategic priorities
- technology maturity and dependencies include explicit uncertainty
- experiments have success and stop criteria
- portfolio ranking considers evidence, value, effort, and risk
- resource scenarios do not autonomously commit budget or people
- executive brief remains subject to human review

Recommended metrics: opportunity-evidence traceability, experiment quality, portfolio calibration, risk recall, executive usefulness, and reviewer override rate.
