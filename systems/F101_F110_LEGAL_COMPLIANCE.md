# F101 to F110: Legal and Compliance Systems

This batch adds ten multi-agent workflow systems for legal-process support, compliance operations, research, evidence organization, and regulatory documentation. These systems are not law firms, do not provide legal advice, and do not make binding legal determinations.

| ID | System | Core roles | Primary output |
|---|---|---|---|
| F101 | Agentic Contract Review | Intake Analyst, Clause Mapper, Risk Spotter, Obligation Tracker, Deviation Reviewer, Counsel Gatekeeper | Contract review package and issue list |
| F102 | Agentic Corporate Compliance | Obligation Mapper, Policy Analyst, Control Reviewer, Evidence Auditor, Gap Analyst, Compliance Gatekeeper | Compliance gap and evidence report |
| F103 | Agentic Privacy Compliance | Data Mapper, Purpose Reviewer, Consent/Notice Analyst, Retention Reviewer, Risk Analyst, Privacy Counsel Gatekeeper | Privacy compliance review package |
| F104 | Agentic Intellectual Property | Asset Intake Agent, Ownership Reviewer, Portfolio Mapper, Risk Reviewer, Evidence Agent, Counsel Gatekeeper | IP portfolio and issue map |
| F105 | Agentic Patent Research | Query Planner, Prior-Art Researcher, Classification Agent, Evidence Reviewer, Claim-Mapping Assistant, Patent Counsel Gatekeeper | Prior-art research package |
| F106 | Agentic Employment Compliance | Policy Intake Agent, Jurisdiction Mapper, Process Reviewer, Documentation Agent, Risk Analyst, Counsel/HR Gatekeeper | Employment-process compliance package |
| F107 | Agentic International Trade | Transaction Mapper, Jurisdiction Agent, Counterparty Reviewer, Documentation Agent, Risk Reviewer, Trade Counsel Gatekeeper | Trade compliance review package |
| F108 | Agentic Export Control | Item/Technology Intake Agent, Classification Support Agent, End-Use Reviewer, Counterparty Reviewer, Evidence Auditor, Export Counsel Gatekeeper | Export-control review package |
| F109 | Agentic Healthcare Compliance | Requirement Mapper, Policy Reviewer, Documentation Agent, Privacy/Security Reviewer, Gap Analyst, Compliance Counsel Gatekeeper | Healthcare compliance evidence package |
| F110 | Agentic Regulatory Affairs | Requirement Analyst, Submission Planner, Evidence Coordinator, Change-Control Reviewer, Gap Analyst, Regulatory Gatekeeper | Regulatory submission readiness package |

## Shared boundary

These workflows organize facts, documents, evidence, obligations, and review questions. They do not represent that a person or organization is legally compliant, do not replace licensed counsel or qualified compliance professionals, and do not autonomously file, sign, accept, reject, certify, or submit legal or regulatory documents.

## Shared engineering requirements

Each system must preserve jurisdiction/context uncertainty, explicit source/evidence status, traceable issue lists, human professional review gates, and clear separation between supplied facts, model-generated observations, and final legal/professional judgment.
