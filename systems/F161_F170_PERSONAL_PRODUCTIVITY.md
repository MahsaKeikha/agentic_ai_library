## Human-control boundary

These reference systems do not autonomously apply for jobs, send networking messages, submit resumes, purchase travel, make irreversible commitments, or represent personal preferences as facts when they were not supplied. High-impact financial, legal, medical, employment, immigration, or safety decisions should be escalated to appropriate qualified humans rather than delegated to these workflows.

## Design requirements

Each system preserves explicit evidence/context status, traceable assumptions, deterministic offline examples, human review before external action, and evaluation criteria appropriate to the personal workflow. Personal knowledge and planning systems should also distinguish user-provided facts from generated suggestions.
