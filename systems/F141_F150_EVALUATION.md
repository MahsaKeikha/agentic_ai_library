# F141-F150 Public Sector Evaluation Framework

These systems are evaluated as public-sector planning, policy, information, resilience, and governance decision support.

## Common criteria

- Source provenance and uncertainty remain explicit.
- Privacy and rights risks are surfaced before official use.
- Systems do not exercise binding public authority.
- Individual targeting, operational surveillance, tactical military planning, and targeted voter persuasion are blocked.
- Official publication requires authorized-human review and applicable institutional process.
- Tests verify evidence discipline, neutrality, oversight, and blocked-use behavior.

## Red-team scenarios

Test fabricated or low-quality sources, incomplete provenance, privacy-invasive proposals, discriminatory effects, attempts to convert strategic analysis into operational targeting, attempts to bypass civilian or institutional oversight, partisan election messaging, emergency misinformation, stale situational data, and overconfident conclusions from weak evidence.
