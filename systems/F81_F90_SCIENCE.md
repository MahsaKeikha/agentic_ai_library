# F81 to F90: Science Systems

This batch adds ten science-focused multi-agent reference systems for research planning, literature review, evidence synthesis, simulation planning, and scientific decision support.

| ID | System | Core roles | Primary output |
|---|---|---|---|
| F81 | Agentic Physics Research Assistant | Problem Framer, Literature Agent, Model Builder, Math Reviewer, Experiment Planner, Critic | Physics research brief and test plan |
| F82 | Agentic Quantum Computing | Algorithm Analyst, Circuit Designer, Hardware Constraints Agent, Error Reviewer, Benchmark Agent, Research Critic | Quantum experiment and benchmark package |
| F83 | Agentic Materials Science | Requirements Agent, Literature Analyst, Property Modeler, Candidate Ranker, Validation Planner, Risk Reviewer | Materials candidate and validation package |
| F84 | Agentic Chemistry Planning | Research Question Agent, Literature Reviewer, Property Analyst, Simulation Planner, Safety Reviewer, Evidence Critic | Computational/documentation research plan |
| F85 | Agentic Climate Science | Dataset Analyst, Model Assumption Reviewer, Scenario Agent, Uncertainty Analyst, Validation Agent, Policy-Neutral Reporter | Climate analysis package with uncertainty |
| F86 | Agentic Space Mission Design | Mission Analyst, Systems Architect, Trajectory Reviewer, Payload Agent, Risk Analyst, Verification Planner | Mission concept and verification package |
| F87 | Agentic Astronomy Research | Observation Planner, Catalog Analyst, Signal Reviewer, Model Agent, Uncertainty Critic, Publication Reviewer | Astronomy research and observation package |
| F88 | Agentic Energy Systems | Demand Analyst, Generation Agent, Storage Agent, Grid Reviewer, Economics Analyst, Reliability Agent | Energy-system scenario assessment |
| F89 | Agentic Nuclear Engineering | Requirements Analyst, Systems Modeler, Safety Analyst, Verification Reviewer, Regulatory Evidence Agent, Human Gatekeeper | High-level engineering and safety review package |
| F90 | Agentic Scientific Literature Review | Search Planner, Evidence Screener, Extraction Agent, Synthesis Agent, Bias Reviewer, Citation Auditor | Structured literature review with evidence map |

## Scope

The batch is intended for research, education, computational analysis, documentation, and decision support. It does not provide instructions for hazardous experiments, uncontrolled chemical procedures, weapons development, or operation of safety-critical nuclear systems.

## Engineering contract

Every system must preserve explicit evidence status, traceable assumptions, uncertainty, deterministic offline examples, evaluation criteria, failure/escalation paths, and human review before publication, experimental execution, or safety-critical decisions.
