# Agentic AI Readiness Assessment

## Know whether your agentic system is actually ready for production

Agentic AI can move from useful assistant to consequential operator very quickly. The hard question is no longer only whether an agent can complete a task. It is whether the full system can be trusted, evaluated, observed, governed, and kept within appropriate human authority.

The **Agentic AI Readiness Assessment** is an evidence-based review of a company's multi-agent or agentic AI system against the **Agentic AI Gold Standard v1.0**.

### What we assess

We examine ten dimensions:

1. agent specialization and responsibility boundaries
2. orchestration and workflow control
3. deterministic tools and structured state
4. memory and context governance
5. evaluation and held-out testing
6. observability and failure visibility
7. safety, security, and privacy
8. human authority and protected actions
9. provenance, evidence, and auditability
10. lifecycle governance and production operations

### What you receive

A typical assessment produces:

- executive readiness score out of 100
- maturity classification
- architecture and agent inventory
- tool and authority map
- protected-action analysis
- evaluation and observability gap analysis
- safety, security, privacy, and memory findings
- production blockers
- prioritized P0 to P3 remediation roadmap
- Gold Standard gap analysis
- executive readout and technical findings review

### Maturity bands

| Score | Readiness |
|---:|---|
| 0 to 39 | Experimental |
| 40 to 59 | Emerging |
| 60 to 74 | Managed |
| 75 to 89 | Production Candidate |
| 90 to 100 | Gold Standard Candidate |

A numerical score never overrides a critical blocker. Systems with uncontrolled consequential actions, missing authorization boundaries, critical security exposure, fabricated evidence, or other serious unresolved risks are not treated as production-ready simply because their aggregate score is high.

### Engagement structure

**1. Discovery**  
Define the system, business objective, users, operating environment, risk surface, and assessment scope.

**2. Evidence review**  
Review architecture, agent roles, prompts and policies, tools, permissions, memory, evaluations, logs, incident controls, deployment process, and available governance evidence.

**3. Technical assessment**  
Score the ten Gold Standard dimensions and identify blockers, authority gaps, failure modes, and missing evidence.

**4. Remediation roadmap**  
Prioritize findings into P0 critical blockers, P1 production-readiness gaps, P2 maturity improvements, and P3 optimizations.

**5. Executive readout**  
Translate technical findings into business risk, decision rights, deployment implications, and next actions.

### The core question

> Your agents may be capable. Are they governable?

The goal is not to slow down agentic AI adoption. The goal is to help organizations move faster with clearer evidence, stronger controls, and explicit human authority.
