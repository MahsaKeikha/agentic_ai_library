## Executive context

[Client] is evaluating an agentic / multi-agent system for [business objective]. The purpose of this engagement is to determine whether the system's engineering and governance controls are sufficiently mature for its intended use and to identify the highest-priority actions required before broader deployment.

## Objectives

- Establish an evidence-based readiness baseline.
- Map agent, tool, memory, orchestration, and authority boundaries.
- Identify production blockers and material control gaps.
- Evaluate testing, observability, security, provenance, and lifecycle practices.
- Produce a prioritized remediation roadmap and executive decision package.

## Scope

The assessment covers the ten Agentic AI Gold Standard pillars: specialization, orchestration, deterministic controls, memory governance, evaluation, observability, safety and security, human authority, provenance, and lifecycle governance.
