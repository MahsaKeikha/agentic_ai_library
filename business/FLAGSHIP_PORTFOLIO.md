# Founding Flagship Portfolio

## Decision

The public portfolio will lead with 10 flagship systems selected from the 170-system Multi-Agent AI Atlas. The 10 systems are the commercial and demonstration front door. The complete Atlas remains the discovery and credibility layer.

## Scoring model

Each dimension is scored from 0 to 20 for a total of 100.

| Dimension | Decision question |
|---|---|
| Market demand | Do identifiable buyers have an urgent and funded problem? |
| Differentiation | Does the system stand apart from generic assistant products? |
| Implementation readiness | Can the architecture support a credible working demonstration? |
| Demonstration value | Can the value and governance be understood visually within minutes? |
| Enterprise potential | Can the system lead to assessment, licensing, implementation, or advisory revenue? |

## Portfolio logic

- F36, F37, and F09 establish technical authority in orchestration, evaluation, and safety.
- F52 and F59 connect the Atlas to health technology, clinical research, caregiver burden, and human-centered design.
- F101 demonstrates governed AI in a high-value professional workflow.
- F98 provides an immediately understandable research and education demonstration.
- F116, F117, and F118 create an industrial intelligence collection aligned with controls, automation, connected systems, and high-value enterprise implementation.

## Flagship completion gate

A system is not launch-ready merely because it is listed here. Each flagship must have:

1. a specific synthetic or public scenario
2. working multi-agent orchestration
3. visible tool, state, memory, and evidence behavior
4. at least one failure or adversarial scenario
5. a protected action requiring human approval
6. deterministic tests and held-out evaluation cases
7. traceable outputs and an observability record
8. a visual architecture diagram
9. a three-minute demonstration script
10. a commercial case study with a clear buyer and measurable value hypothesis

## Commercial role

The flagships create proof for the service ladder already defined in the commercialization plan:

- executive and team workshops
- Agentic AI Readiness Assessment
- Gold Standard Gap Review
- Multi-Agent Architecture Sprint
- Evaluation and Governance Program
- enterprise implementation
- ongoing advisory

The public message remains: the Atlas is a professional engineering library and governance framework, not a prompt collection.
