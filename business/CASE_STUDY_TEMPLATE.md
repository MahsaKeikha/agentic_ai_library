## Verified results

Only include metrics supported by evidence.

Possible metrics:

- evaluation pass rate before and after
- number of uncontrolled protected actions removed
- percentage of tools moved to least privilege
- trace coverage
- mean time to identify failed workflows
- number of critical blockers closed
- regression rate
- human-approval coverage for consequential actions
- deployment lead time
- incident frequency

Avoid vague claims such as `dramatically safer` unless the evidence and definition are provided.
