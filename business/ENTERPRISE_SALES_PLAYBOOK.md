# Agentic AI Gold Standard Enterprise Sales Playbook

## Funnel

Website visitor -> Readiness Scorecard -> Assessment Inquiry -> Qualification -> Discovery -> Readiness Assessment -> Remediation / Architecture Sprint -> Ongoing Advisory.

## Lead qualification

The website assigns an internal routing class from non-sensitive intake data.

- **A - Priority:** near-term need, material deployment maturity, and/or meaningful organizational complexity. Respond first.
- **B - Qualified:** credible assessment opportunity that merits discovery.
- **C - Nurture:** early-stage or exploratory. Provide useful material and revisit when deployment becomes concrete.

Qualification is a sales-routing heuristic, not a statement about technical quality, risk, compliance, or customer value.

## Response targets

- A - Priority: respond within one business day.
- B - Qualified: respond within two business days.
- C - Nurture: respond with the assessment overview and invite future re-engagement.

## 30-minute discovery call

1. Business objective and decision that the system supports.
2. Current deployment stage and intended production timeline.
3. Agent topology, orchestration, external tools, memory, and protected actions at a high level.
4. Current evaluation, observability, security, and human-approval controls.
5. Primary blocker or executive concern.
6. Evidence available for private review.
7. Confirm assessment scope, stakeholders, access constraints, timeline, and procurement path.

Do not request secrets, credentials, regulated personal data, or unnecessary production data during discovery.

## Offer ladder

### Enterprise Readiness Assessment
Evidence-based review against the Agentic AI Gold Standard. Typical commercial range: **$10,000-$25,000**, subject to scope, system complexity, evidence volume, stakeholder count, and required domain review.

### Gold Standard Gap Review
Focused review of known maturity gaps and a prioritized remediation plan. Price by scope.

### Architecture Sprint
Hands-on design of agents, orchestration, tools, state, memory, evaluation, observability, security, and authority. Price by scope and duration.

### Evaluation and Governance Program
Build repeatable evaluation, traceability, approval, release, and incident infrastructure. Price by program scope.

### Ongoing Advisory
Recurring architecture and governance support. Use a monthly retainer based on expected access and review volume.

## Sales stages

1. New inquiry
2. Qualified
3. Discovery scheduled
4. Discovery complete
5. Scope confirmed
6. Proposal sent
7. Procurement / legal
8. Won
9. Lost / nurture

## Required CRM fields

Company, contact, role, work email, industry, company size, deployment stage, desired timeline, agent count, external-tool count, readiness score/band, qualification class, primary concern, discovery date, proposed offer, estimated value, next action, next-action date, status.

## Evidence handling

The public form is for high-level information only. Confidential architecture evidence should be exchanged after discovery using a mutually approved private channel. Access should be minimized to what is necessary for the assessment.
