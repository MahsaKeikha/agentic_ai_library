CLAIM BOUNDARIES

What the work does not promise.

  • No guarantee that a system is safe, secure, compliant, or error free
  • No regulatory certification, legal opinion, penetration test, or audit opinion
  • No production claim without organization-specific evidence
  • No replacement for qualified legal, clinical, security, privacy, or regulatory review
  • No transfer of consequential authority away from the accountable organization
