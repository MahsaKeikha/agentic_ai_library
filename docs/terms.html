TERMS OF USE

A public engineering reference, with explicit limits.

These terms describe the intended use of the Atlas website, simulations, scorecards, field guide, and related public materials.

Effective August 24, 2026

Educational and engineering reference

The Atlas provides reference architectures, synthetic scenarios, engineering frameworks, and public demonstrations. It does not provide legal, medical, financial, regulatory, cybersecurity, or other licensed professional advice.

No certification or guarantee

Project maturity labels and readiness scores are defined within the Atlas framework. They are not regulatory certifications, legal compliance determinations, safety guarantees, audit opinions, or proof of production deployment.

Human responsibility

Visitors remain responsible for their own decisions, implementations, testing, permissions, and professional review. Public examples must not be used to authorize consequential actions without appropriate organization-specific controls and accountable human approval.

Synthetic demonstrations

Demo Lab and Atlas Challenge scenarios are synthetic educational experiences. They do not control external systems and do not establish real-world performance.

Repository licenses

Code, documentation, and other repository materials are governed by the license and notices published with the relevant repository. Third-party components retain their own licenses and terms.

No engagement through browsing

Browsing the website, submitting a scorecard, or sending an inquiry does not create a consulting, legal, fiduciary, partnership, or other professional relationship. Any paid work requires mutually accepted scope and terms.

Responsible use

Do not use the public website to submit secrets, credentials, sensitive personal data, protected health information, confidential records, or unlawful content. Do not attempt to interfere with the website or misrepresent the Atlas as a certification authority.

Updates

Public materials may change as the repository, evidence, and services evolve. Version history and repository records provide additional change context.
