        03 / THE ENGINEERING QUESTION

        The hard problem is not whether AI can make decisions.

        It is deciding which actions may be autonomous when communications are delayed, which require local human authority, and how evidence and conflicting objectives are preserved when the system is under stress.

        01

        Resource conflicts are first class

        Power, robotics, science, thermal control, communications, and habitat survival cannot all optimize independently. F36 must arbitrate across shared constraints.

        02

        Failure is visible

        Bad sensors, stale twin state, missing inventory, contradictory recommendations, or unsafe instructions remain explicit blockers instead of being hidden by fluent output.

        03

        Autonomy is bounded

        The simulation allows reversible predefined operations while keeping crew safety limits, interlock overrides, human EVA, and irreversible mission actions outside autonomous authority.

        04

        Every decision has provenance

        The event trace records agents, tools, evidence, conflicts, gates, and final authority so a reviewer can reconstruct why the system acted or refused to act.
