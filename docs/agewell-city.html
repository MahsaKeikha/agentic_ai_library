ATLAS FLAGSHIP / HUMAN CENTERED AGING

AGEWELL CITY + INDEPENDENT AND ASSISTED LIVING

What if growing older meant gaining support without giving up independence?

AGEWELL CITY is an agentic AI operating layer for independent living, assisted living, accessible mobility, home safety, caregiver coordination, community services and age friendly neighborhoods. The resident remains a person with preferences, consent and authority, not a collection of sensor readings.

WHY AGEWELL CITY EXISTS

Aging well is not one healthcare problem.

Independence depends on many systems working together: the home, transportation, buildings, caregivers, community spaces, maintenance, accessibility, social connection and health access. Most technology sees these as separate products. AGEWELL CITY treats them as one human experience while keeping each system inside a clear authority boundary.

01

Support the life someone wants

The system starts from resident preferences and consent rather than maximizing monitoring.

02

Notice without diagnosing

A change in routine can trigger a gentle check in or evidence request. It is not silently converted into a medical conclusion.

03

Coordinate the care circle

Family, caregivers, staff, transport and community services can share the right information without everyone receiving everything.

04

Preserve the right to say no

Convenience does not erase consent. The resident can remain the decision maker wherever capacity and law allow.

      04 / HUMAN CENTERED AGING CHARTER

      Technology should expand a person's life, not shrink it into a safety score.

      The flagship is designed around independence, dignity, privacy, relationships and qualified human care. Efficiency matters, but it is not the highest goal.

      01

      Consent before sensing

      Collect less. Explain why. Let people understand what is used and who can see it.

      02

      Support without surveillance

      Age friendly technology should not require permanent identity tracking or constant observation.

      03

      Human relationships remain real

      AI can reduce coordination burden, but it should not pretend that companionship, trust and care are software functions.

      04

      Clinical authority stays clinical

      Medication, diagnosis, treatment and emergency judgment stay with qualified people and authorized services.

      05

      Accessibility is infrastructure

      Mobility, hearing, vision, cognition and language needs should shape transportation, buildings and digital services from the beginning.

      06

      Independence is a measurable outcome

      Success includes time at home, reliable access, fewer avoidable disruptions, better caregiver coordination and stronger participation in community life.

      05 / FROM FLAGSHIP TO REAL WORLD

      Start with one age friendly community, not an entire healthcare system.

      The first real pilot can connect a senior living community, residential development, campus style retirement community, mixed age neighborhood or assisted living campus to one shared coordination layer.

      REAL WORLD PILOT

      AGEWELL 01

      A deployable intelligence layer for independent and assisted living that starts read only, validates data quality and consent, then adds a small set of reversible support actions.

      01

      Connect

      Smart home environment, call systems, building access, shuttle status, maintenance, community schedules and optional wearables where residents choose them.

      02

      Coordinate

      Create a shared resident and community state without collapsing every data source into one unrestricted profile.

      03

      Support

      Generate check ins, accessible routing, transport requests, maintenance coordination and caregiver information with explicit role boundaries.

      04

      Measure

      Track response time, resident choice, accessibility, caregiver burden, service reliability, false alarms and blocked unsafe actions.

      FIRST INTEGRATIONS
      • building and apartment environmental sensors
      • resident call or check in systems
      • accessible shuttle and transport status
      • elevator and facility access state
      • maintenance and work order systems
      • community event and service schedules
      • optional resident wearables with explicit consent
      • authorized caregiver and staff communication
      WHAT WE DO NOT START WITH
      • autonomous diagnosis
      • medication changes
      • continuous camera surveillance
      • unrestricted location tracking
      • automatic emergency declarations
      • removing resident access or choice
      • high consequence automation without validation

      Build a place where independence is part of the infrastructure.

      AGEWELL CITY can become the senior living and age friendly layer of a larger smart city program, or stand alone as a focused community pilot.

      06 / A FLAGSHIP FOR LONGER LIVES

      Future cities should be designed for a 90 year life, not only a 30 year old commuter.

      AGEWELL CITY is relevant to new master planned communities, retirement destinations, mixed age districts, assisted living operators, healthcare campuses, real estate developers and governments building age friendly infrastructure.

      INDEPENDENT LIVING

      Live at home longer with the right support around you.

      Coordinate the environment, transport, maintenance, community access and care circle without turning the home into a surveillance room.

      ASSISTED LIVING

      Give staff better coordination, not another alert dashboard.

      Bring building state, mobility needs, transport, service requests and resident preferences into one operating picture while clinical judgment remains human.

      AGE FRIENDLY CITY

      Make accessibility and aging part of city intelligence.

      Heat response, cooling access, accessible routes, public spaces, transport and community services can coordinate around older residents without isolating them into a separate city.
