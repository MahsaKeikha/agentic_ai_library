# Public Adoption Registry

## Purpose

This registry records verifiable use of the Multi-Agent AI Atlas in research, teaching, engineering evaluation, and organizational practice. It exists to prevent informal interest from being presented as adoption.

## Evidence required

An entry requires at least one of the following:

- a public course, syllabus, workshop, paper, article, or repository that cites the Atlas
- written permission from an organization to name the use and its scope
- a public implementation or evaluation artifact linked to a specific Atlas system
- a signed case-study approval describing what may be disclosed

## Registry fields

| Organization or project | Use category | Atlas system or artifact | Evidence link | Date verified | Public boundary |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|

No public adoption entry is added without evidence and permission appropriate to the claim.

## Claim boundaries

- An inquiry is not adoption.
- A demonstration is not deployment.
- A training attendee is not an organizational endorsement.
- A prototype is not a production system.
- A citation is evidence of use, not proof of agreement with every Atlas claim.

Confidential engagements may remain unlisted. Their absence from this public registry should not be interpreted as either adoption or non-adoption.
