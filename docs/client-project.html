01 / LEAD TO SCOPE

What we establish before writing agent code.

The business project starts with workflow evidence, operational constraints, measurable outcomes, and decision authority, not with choosing a model.

BUSINESS OUTCOME

Resolve routine service cases faster

Target damage, return, shipment, and low-value refund requests that currently consume human handling time.

CONNECTED SYSTEMS

Zendesk + Shopify + Stripe + policy knowledge

The production version would use least-privilege adapters. This public room uses local synthetic connectors with the same interface shape.

PROTECTED ACTION

Refund issuance and customer communication

During the pilot, the AI can investigate, calculate, recommend, and draft. A human must authorize the protected action.

SUCCESS CRITERIA

Quality, speed, containment, traceability

Measure resolution time, policy accuracy, escalation rate, unsafe-action blocks, reviewer agreement, and full provenance.

        05 / CLIENT DELIVERY PLAN

        What we would deliver in an actual paid engagement.

        The project becomes a sequence of evidence-backed milestones rather than an open-ended AI experiment.

        WEEK 1

        Discovery + evidence map

        Workflow inventory, decision rights, systems, data classes, baseline metrics, failure consequences.

        WEEK 2

        Architecture + evaluation contract

        Agent responsibilities, tool scopes, state schema, routing, human gates, acceptance tests.

        WEEK 3

        Connected prototype

        Sandbox adapters, retrieval, policy layer, workflow orchestration, observability, audit trace.

        WEEK 4

        Adversarial evaluation

        Edge cases, injection, missing evidence, conflicting policy, tool failures, escalation tests.

        WEEK 5

        Controlled pilot

        Limited users, human approval, quality monitoring, rollback criteria, daily review.

        WEEK 6

        Go / no-go package

        Business metrics, technical evidence, unresolved risks, operating ownership, next-stage roadmap.
