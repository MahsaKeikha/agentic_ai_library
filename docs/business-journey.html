WHAT YOU JUST SAW

Technical words translated into business language.

You do not need to become an AI engineer. You need to understand who does what, what information they can use, what they are allowed to do, and when a person must decide.

LLM
The language reasoning capability used by an AI role.
Agent
A digital specialist with one job, instructions, tools, and boundaries.
Multi-agent system
Several digital specialists coordinating to complete one larger job.
Human in the loop
A named person reviews, corrects, approves, or stops important actions.

YOUR FIRST RESPONSIBLE STEP

Do not begin by buying “AI.” Begin with one business job.

Choose a repetitive, measurable process. Define the information it may use, the actions it may suggest, and the decisions that must remain human. Then test it in a bounded pilot.
