AI ENGINEERING HANDBOOK SERIES

Three volumes. One engineering path from foundations to production intelligence.

Written by Mahsa Keikha, PhD, the AI Engineering Handbook Series is designed for engineers, technical leaders, researchers, students, and builders who want to understand not only how modern AI works, but how to engineer reliable systems around it. The series progresses from core AI concepts to agentic systems, production architecture, specialized agents, and multi-agent engineering.

Volume 1Understand the foundations and the core components that modern AI systems are built from.
Volume 2Move from models to agentic AI, orchestration, engineering practice, and real system design.
Volume 3Engineer production AI, specialized agents, multi-agent architecture, and advanced system expansion.

Where the Atlas fits.

The Multi-Agent AI Atlas is a living engineering companion to the ideas developed across the handbook series. The books explain the principles and engineering progression. The Atlas lets readers inspect architectures, compare multi-agent systems, follow agent handoffs, examine evidence, and see where human authority must remain final. Together they connect technical learning with visible system design.
