# 90-Day Commercialization Roadmap

## Objective

Turn the Agentic AI Library from a strong open engineering reference into a repeatable business-development engine for agentic AI assessment, architecture, governance, implementation, and training.

The library remains the public credibility layer. Commercial offerings solve organization-specific problems that require analysis, customization, evidence review, implementation support, or ongoing governance.

## Commercial thesis

Do not sell 170 separate agents.

Sell a trusted capability around:

**Design it. Test it. Govern it. Operationalize it.**

The open library demonstrates breadth. The Gold Standard demonstrates methodology. The Enterprise Readiness Assessment creates a repeatable entry service. Client work reveals which capabilities should later become software.

## Phase 1: Package the offer | Days 1-15

### Required assets

Complete and publish:
- Agentic AI Gold Standard v1.0
- Enterprise Readiness Assessment
- Enterprise Services page
- one-page assessment overview
- one sample anonymized assessment report
- one executive briefing deck
- one technical architecture deck
- standard discovery questionnaire
- statement of work template

### Initial service ladder

Pricing below should be treated as market-testing hypotheses, not guaranteed market prices.

| Offer | Initial pricing hypothesis | Goal |
|---|---:|---|
| 60-minute executive workshop | $1,500-$3,500 | Low-friction paid entry |
| Team workshop | $5,000-$15,000 | Education plus discovery |
| Agentic AI Readiness Assessment | $10,000-$25,000 | Core entry offer |
| Gold Standard Gap Review | $15,000-$35,000 | Technical governance review |
| Multi-Agent Architecture Sprint | $20,000-$50,000 | Design engagement |
| Evaluation and Governance Program | $25,000-$75,000 | Higher-value implementation support |
| Enterprise implementation | Custom | Larger transformation work |
| Ongoing advisory | $5,000-$20,000/month | Recurring relationship |

Do not publish all pricing immediately. Use it internally while validating willingness to pay.

## Phase 2: Build proof | Days 16-30

### Produce three flagship case studies

Use public or synthetic examples so no confidential client data is required.

Recommended examples:

1. Healthcare agent system
   - patient or caregiver workflow
   - strict human authority
   - privacy and clinical boundaries

2. Financial-services agent system
   - protected transactions
   - compliance and auditability
   - fail-closed governance

3. Enterprise operations agent system
   - executive workflow
   - tool permissions
   - multi-agent coordination

Each case study should show:
- original architecture
- risk map
- readiness score
- Gold Standard gaps
- redesigned architecture
- governance improvements
- before/after maturity

## Phase 3: Create demand | Days 31-45

### Publish authority content

Create a recurring content series around a small number of memorable ideas.

Recommended themes:
- Why agentic AI fails open
- Human authority in autonomous workflows
- 10 failure modes of multi-agent systems
- Why orchestration is not governance
- What an enterprise should require before giving an AI agent tools
- How to evaluate agent systems beyond accuracy
- Agentic AI readiness for boards
- What L3 Gold Standard means

### Content funnel

Every major article should lead to one clear action:

**Request an Agentic AI Readiness Assessment**

Do not create a different CTA for every post.

## Phase 4: Recruit design partners | Days 46-60

Target 20-30 organizations where agentic AI is already being built or seriously evaluated.

Good target profiles:
- AI-native startups
- digital-health companies
- financial-technology companies
- robotics companies
- enterprise software companies
- regulated-industry innovation teams
- consulting firms needing agent-governance expertise
- universities or research labs deploying multi-agent systems

### Design-partner offer

Offer a bounded engagement with a clear deliverable rather than free consulting.

Example:

**Agentic AI Readiness Design Partner**

Includes:
- 90-minute architecture discovery
- evidence review
- readiness scoring
- top 10 findings
- protected-action map
- 30-day remediation roadmap
- executive debrief

Discounting is optional, but never make the work undefined or unlimited.

## Phase 5: Convert patterns into product | Days 61-90

After several assessments, identify repeated manual work.

Likely product candidates:
- agent registry
- tool-permission map
- protected-action registry
- evaluation scenario manager
- governance scorecard
- human-approval workflow
- provenance viewer
- agent dependency graph
- maturity dashboard
- audit export

Only build software for workflows that repeat across multiple real engagements.

## Flagship future product concept

### Agentic AI Control Plane

A governance and observability layer for agentic systems.

Potential modules:

```text
Agent Registry
Tool and Permission Map
Workflow Graph
Memory Boundaries
Protected Actions
Evaluation Suites
Readiness Score
Human Approval Gates
Observability
Incident History
Audit Trail
```

The first version does not need to execute agents. It can begin as an assessment and governance layer around systems organizations already have.

## Sales narrative

Avoid:

"We have 170 AI agents."

Prefer:

"We built 170 reference architectures to study a harder problem: how agentic systems should be orchestrated, evaluated, governed, and kept accountable to human authority across different domains. We now use that framework to assess and design enterprise agent systems."

## Metrics for the first 90 days

Track:
- qualified conversations
- assessment proposals sent
- assessment conversion rate
- average engagement value
- paid workshops
- design partners
- repeat engagements
- inbound leads from GitHub
- inbound leads from thought leadership
- number of recurring assessment tasks that could become software

Do not optimize primarily for GitHub stars. Stars support credibility. Revenue comes from solving urgent organizational problems.

## Revenue scenario planning

Use scenarios rather than forecasts presented as certainty.

### Conservative validation scenario
- 2 readiness assessments at $10K
- 2 workshops at $5K
- 1 architecture sprint at $20K
- total: $50K

### Strong early traction scenario
- 4 readiness assessments at $20K
- 2 workshops at $10K
- 2 architecture or governance engagements at $40K
- total: $180K

### Enterprise traction scenario
- 4 assessments at $25K
- 3 implementation or governance engagements at $75K
- 2 ongoing advisory retainers at $10K/month for 3 months
- total: $385K

These are planning scenarios only. Actual results depend on demand, positioning, buyer urgency, credibility, sales execution, competition, and delivery quality.

## What not to do

Avoid:
- building dozens of new agents before validating buyers
- giving away unlimited custom consulting
- making regulatory certification claims
- claiming the Gold Standard guarantees safety
- pricing only by hours
- trying to sell to every industry at once
- building a large SaaS platform before seeing repeated customer needs

## Near-term priority

The next commercial milestone is not another repository.

It is the first paid enterprise assessment using the framework.

Once that is repeatable, the library becomes more than a portfolio. It becomes the top of a business system.
