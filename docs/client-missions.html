What is real in these public demos: the architecture, agent separation, state transitions, synthetic tool interfaces, failure logic, evidence trace, gating logic, and interactive runtime are executable in the browser. What is intentionally not claimed: the public rooms do not connect to a real client's private systems, real patients, live machinery, real payment credentials, or production model APIs. A client engagement replaces the synthetic adapters with approved server-side integrations and organization-specific evidence.

WHY THREE ROOMS

Different leaders need to see themselves in the system.

The commerce room explains the value in everyday business language. The clinical room demonstrates traceability and fail-closed governance. The industrial room demonstrates cyber-physical boundaries and engineering evidence.
