What is real in these public demos: the architecture, agent separation, state transitions, synthetic tool interfaces, failure logic, evidence trace, gating logic, and interactive runtime are executable in the browser. What is intentionally not claimed: the public rooms do not connect to a real client's private systems, real patients, live machinery, payment credentials, confidential research corpora, or production model APIs. A client engagement replaces synthetic adapters with approved server-side integrations and organization-specific evidence.

WHY FOUR ROOMS

Different leaders need to see themselves in the system.

The commerce room explains everyday business value. Clinical demonstrates traceability and fail-closed governance. Industrial demonstrates cyber-physical boundaries. Research demonstrates evidence provenance, citation integrity, and separation between AI assistance and scholarly responsibility.

BUSINESS OWNER

Understand value and workflow

See where AI reduces repetitive work, where it needs evidence, and where a person still owns the decision.

AI / SOFTWARE ENGINEER

Inspect orchestration and state

Follow agents, routes, tool calls, structured events, blockers, evidence, and explicit approval boundaries.

RISK / COMPLIANCE / RESEARCH LEADER

See failure and escalation

Run cases where the system refuses to continue because consent, safety, provenance, citation, or evidence requirements are unresolved.

OPERATIONS / R&D LEADER

Connect architecture to outcomes

Use editable assumptions to discuss capacity, risk exposure, pilot scope, research quality, and measurable success criteria.
