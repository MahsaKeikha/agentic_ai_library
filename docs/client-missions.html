What is real in these public demos: the architecture, agent separation, state transitions, synthetic tool interfaces, failure logic, evidence trace, gating logic, and interactive runtime are executable in the browser. What is intentionally not claimed: the public rooms do not connect to private client systems, real patients, live machinery, payment credentials, confidential research corpora, spacecraft, launch vehicles, command uplinks, or production model APIs. A client engagement replaces synthetic adapters with approved server-side integrations and organization-specific evidence.

WHY FIVE ROOMS

Different leaders need to see themselves in the system.

Commerce explains everyday business value. Clinical demonstrates fail-closed governance. Industrial demonstrates cyber-physical boundaries. Research demonstrates evidence provenance. Space demonstrates systems engineering, interacting margins, fault protection, and strict separation between advisory AI and flight authority.
