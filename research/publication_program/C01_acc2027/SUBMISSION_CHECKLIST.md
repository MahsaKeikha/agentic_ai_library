## Novelty wording to preserve

Do not claim that supervisory control, runtime assurance, temporal tool constraints, or human oversight are themselves new. The paper's novelty claim is scoped to the Authority-Evidence Task Automaton formulation, explicit protected-action/human-review semantics, synthesized predecessor-aware nonblocking supervisor for agentic workflows, and the accompanying reproducible benchmark/oracle validation.
