# Publication Lineage: Research Family 1

## Conference baseline

**C01**
Anticipatory Supervisory Control of Protected Actions in Tool-Using Multi-Agent AI

Primary venue: American Control Conference 2027

Role of C01:
- establish the compact control problem;
- define AETA and AESC;
- define precursor vulnerability;
- prove the conference-level safe and nonblocking results;
- demonstrate exact reachability behavior;
- provide reproducible finite-state experiments and scaling.

C01 is the first publication in this lineage.

## Frozen-baseline rule

At submission, preserve:
- final conference PDF;
- exact LaTeX source;
- all figure sources;
- code;
- tests;
- raw and summarized results;
- result manifest;
- reference audit;
- submission metadata;
- repository commit SHA.

The journal paper must treat this material as prior work and cite it explicitly.

## Journal extension

**J01**
Compositional Runtime Assurance for Heterogeneous Agentic AI Systems Under Human Authority

Primary target: IEEE Transactions on Automatic Control, subject to final scope and contribution quality.

J01 must add substantial material beyond C01. Candidate journal-only contributions include:
- compositional multi-agent contracts;
- partial observation;
- asynchronous handoffs;
- dynamic authority grant and revocation;
- evidence-age state dynamics;
- delayed human-review availability;
- multiple protected-action classes;
- correlated failures;
- dynamic supervisor updates;
- richer integrations with F36, F117/F118, AGEWELL CITY, and Smart City Agentic AI;
- expanded proofs, baselines, ablations, robustness studies, and scalability experiments.

## Overlap rule

The journal paper must not:
- copy C01 as a minimally expanded manuscript;
- present C01 results as new;
- reuse conference figures as if they were new journal evidence;
- obscure the conference lineage.

The journal paper must:
- cite C01;
- identify which material is inherited;
- state the substantive journal additions;
- include new results sufficient to support a distinct journal contribution.

## Sequence

1. Finalize and submit C01.
2. Freeze the exact conference baseline.
3. Record reviewer feedback when available.
4. Open a dedicated J01 development branch.
5. Build the new journal-only theory and experiments.
6. Perform a fresh novelty and overlap audit.
7. Submit J01 only after the extension is independently mature.
